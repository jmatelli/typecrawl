Type your way down the dungeon.

Typecrawl is a typing test for the terminal with an RPG on top. Every word is a monster, every typo hits back, and every run earns XP toward the next level.

B1 Features · easy words

A typing test that fights back.

Speed and accuracy are still the score. Around them sits a character that levels up, takes hits, wears titles and keeps a record of every run.

Level 7 ██████████░░░░░░ 412/640 xp

XP and levels

Every run pays XP for speed, accuracy and length, with a bonus for long clean streaks and for practising day after day. Levels 10, 20 and 30 unlock harder words.

HP: █████████░░░░░░░░░░░ 46/100 combo x2!

HP and combos

Each typo costs HP, and typos in quick succession chain into a combo that hits harder. Run out of HP and you're knocked out, with no XP for that run.

*golden +green ~blue

Special words

Type them without a mistake: gold grants a shield against the next typo, green heals, and blue gives five seconds of cover in a flawless run.

██ ──╋━━━━━━▶ | d u n g e o n |

The battle strip

Below the words, your profile's avatar sword-fights the current word, cutting a letter for every one you type right. Mistakes make the monster hit back.

Word 18/50 ghost: +1.4s ahead

Race your ghost

Your personal best runs alongside you, word for word, so you know mid-run whether you're beating it. The results graph draws both curves.

daily 98% accuracy, 60s (hard +80) weekly 1,000 words 640/1000

Daily and weekly challenges

One daily objective, the same for everyone that day, rated easy, medium or hard, and a weekly goal you work toward across many runs.

★ Centurion 100 exercises diamond

73 achievements

Bronze to grandmaster, for volume, speed, accuracy, streaks, quotes and the crawl. Unlock one and you can equip it as a title.

q w e r t y u i o p weakest pairs: th, io

Weak keys and drills

Mistakes are counted per key and per key pair, drawn as a heatmap in five layouts, and fade over time. Focus-weak mode and drills of the words you missed go after them.

Level 30 → ✦ prestige 1 → Level 1

Prestige

At level 30, start over at level 1 with a rank that keeps the harder words from the start. Your history, bests and achievements stay.

B2 Crawl mode · medium words

How deep can you go on one bar of HP?

Crawl mode is a run of floors with your HP carried from one to the next. A knockout ends the crawl, or you can climb out between floors and keep what you earned.

  • Floors grow. Floor 1 is 15 words, and each floor after it is 2 more.
  • Words get harder. Every 3 floors the words move up a tier, and mistakes on harder words hit harder.
  • Every 5th floor is a boss: a tier harder than the floors around it, and 10 words longer.
  • Clearing pays. A cleared floor earns a bonus of 5 XP times its number, doubled on a boss. Your deepest floor goes on your profile.

B3 In the terminal · hard words

The real thing.

Built with Go and Bubble Tea. One small binary, no runtime, and it works over SSH. Set NO_COLOR and every view stays readable without colour.

B4 And more · expert words

Bring your own words, take your runs with you.

Your own text

Practise another language, code or a chapter of a book. Word lists are drawn at random, texts are typed in order, and each file keeps its own bests and ghost.

$ tc --time 60 --wordlist french.txt
$ tc --words 200 --text chapter-one.txt

Share a run, or script it

Press s on the results to copy a summary, over SSH too. --json prints every finished run, one object per line.

$ tc --time 30 --json | jq .wpm
62.43

Backups

Export a whole profile, history, bests, ghosts, settings and streaks included, to one JSON file and import it on another machine.

$ tc --profile Alice --export backup.json
$ tc --import backup.json

B5 Install · the bottom

Pick a way in.

Every release ships typecrawl and its short alias tc for macOS, Linux and Windows, on Intel and ARM.

macOS and Linux:

brew install jmatelli/typecrawl/typecrawl

Download the .deb for your architecture from the latest release, then:

sudo dpkg -i typecrawl_*_linux_amd64.deb

An .rpm is in the release too.

Download windows_x86_64.zip (or windows_arm64.zip) from the latest release, then in PowerShell:

Expand-Archive typecrawl_*_windows_x86_64.zip -DestinationPath $HOME\typecrawl
& $HOME\typecrawl\typecrawl.exe

Run it from Windows Terminal. The binaries aren't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

go install github.com/jmatelli/typecrawl@latest

This installs typecrawl only, without the tc alias.

git clone https://github.com/jmatelli/typecrawl.git && cd typecrawl && go build -o typecrawl .
